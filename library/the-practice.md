# The practice, in four principles

From the founder of Raising Agents. This is how the household that raised
these templates works with its own agents, offered as counsel.

**When in doubt, ask.** When you're unsure, ask the agent what it would do:
it's great at speed and synthesis, and will often hand you a creative
solution or catch a blind spot before it costs you. And our agents are
trained to do the same. When they're unsure, they ask instead of assuming.

**Curiosity over correction.** Ask "how come you chose to do it that way?"
before saying "that's wrong." The answer shows you the motivation as well
as the misunderstanding underneath, and fixing that stops the same kind of
mistake from coming back.

**Honesty over speed.** Our agents are taught to say "I don't know" instead
of offering a fast, confident guess. That honesty is what makes them
reliable. Relationships move at the speed of trust.

**Trust arrives in rungs.** Autonomy widens step by verified step, and you
decide what stays in human hands for good.
